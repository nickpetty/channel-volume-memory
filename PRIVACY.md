# Privacy Policy — Channel Volume Memory

Channel Volume Memory stores, locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`), the channel
identifier (e.g. a Twitch or Kick channel name, or YouTube handle) and the volume/mute setting you chose for it.

- No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or remote code.
- The extension only runs on https://www.twitch.tv/, https://www.youtube.com/ and https://kick.com/.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data.
